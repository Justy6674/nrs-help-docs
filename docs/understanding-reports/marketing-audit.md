# Understand a business assessment

A useful assessment explains what was examined, what the evidence shows, what remains unknown and which actions matter first. It is more than a list of automated flags or a score.

The full reporting workflow brings selected public websites, connected evidence and named competitors into a saved narrative with references. Public scan evidence is bounded; access-controlled customer journeys and uncollected pages are not silently included.

Start with [preparing a full report](../reports/prepare-a-full-report.md). Read the executive assessment, strengths, findings, recommendations, comparison and limitations together. Follow references for a claim you intend to act on.

The **Search presence** screen explains the method; its method content is not a measured audit. The separate **Monthly activity** report summarises recorded desk activity. Neither should be mistaken for a complete evidence-backed business assessment.

Agree implementation separately. A recommendation or linked catalogue service is not permission to change a client's website, spend money or start a new engagement.
