# Measure business value without invented ROI

Start with a measurable business outcome: time saved in a process, qualified enquiries, completed sales or another agreed result. Record the baseline, measurement period and costs included.

Social views, search impressions and GA4 key events are useful signals, but none automatically equals revenue or profit. A key event is configured in the client's analytics property; its meaning needs checking before it is described as a booking or sale.

## Build an honest comparison

Use like-for-like periods and identify changes in the business, tracking or campaign that could affect results. Record the source of each number and distinguish observation from attribution. If a link between the work and the outcome is uncertain, say so.

The current full-report collector does not read patient events, user journeys or revenue from GA4. There is no universal automatic ROI claim in the report.

Agree the evaluation method with NRS at the start of the engagement. A practical improvement with a clear baseline is more useful than an impressive percentage without a source.
