# Generate Marketing Images
*Create custom images for social media, blogs, and ads*

Your Director can generate images using AI. This is useful when you need a quick visual for a social media post, blog article, or ad — without hiring a designer or searching through stock photos.

## How to ask

Use the /visuals shortcut or describe what you want:

1. /visuals followed by a description — for example, /visuals a modern clinic reception area with natural lighting

2. Or just say "Create an image for my Instagram post about summer skincare"

3. Be as descriptive as you like — mention colours, mood, style, and what should be in the image

> **Tip:** The more specific your description, the better the result. "A flat-lay photo of skincare products on a marble surface with eucalyptus leaves" will give a much better image than just "skincare image".

## What you can create

AI image generation works best for:

1. Social media graphics and post images

2. Blog header images and featured images

3. Concept art and mood boards

4. Product lifestyle images (not product photos)

5. Abstract and decorative backgrounds

> **Warning:** AI-generated images should not be used for medical before-and-after photos or to represent real patients. For healthcare businesses, always use real photography for clinical results.

## Using images with your content

When your Director creates a social media post or blog, it may automatically generate a matching image. You can also ask for images separately and pair them with your own content. Generated images are saved to your media library for reuse.

## Design vs image generation

Image generation creates pictures from scratch using AI. For branded graphics with your logo, specific layouts, and text overlays, use the /design command instead — that uses Canva, which gives you more control over the final result.
