# Approve or Reject Posts
*Review content before it goes live*

Every post can be reviewed before publishing. When you or your Director creates a post, it starts as a draft. You can approve it (which schedules it for publishing) or reject it with feedback.

## Approving a post

Click any post in the Creative Studio feed, then click "Approve & Schedule" in the detail panel. The post moves from draft to scheduled and will be published at the set time.

## Rejecting a post

Click "Reject" and optionally add a reason. The post returns to draft status so it can be revised. You can also add comments for feedback without rejecting.

## Comments

The comment thread below each post lets you leave notes and feedback. This is useful for team collaboration — leave a comment and your team member can see it when they review the post.
