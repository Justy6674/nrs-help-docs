# Contact NRS

Email [office@blackhealthintelligence.com](mailto:office@blackhealthintelligence.com), the contact address in NRS's current site configuration, or use the contact arrangement agreed for your engagement.

## Include the essentials

- The business or client workspace.
- What you were trying to finish.
- What happened instead, including safe error text.
- The item link or reference and approximate time.
- Your browser and device if the problem concerns the screen.

For invoices, include the invoice reference and requested correction. For publishing, identify the affected account and existing post rather than creating a replacement. For a report, include its run or report reference and whether the issue concerns preparation, review, PDF or delivery.

Do not include passwords, tokens, card details, patient information or unrelated clients in a screenshot. If the issue affects access, describe it without sharing another person's login.

Response arrangements depend on the engagement. This help centre does not promise a support SLA or emergency clinical assistance.
