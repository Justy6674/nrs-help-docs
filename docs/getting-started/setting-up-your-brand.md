# Set up your brand kit

Open **Settings → Brand kit** for the intended business. Start with facts people will use repeatedly: legal name, website, booking URL, phone and service area. Check links against the actual service before saving.

## Add a usable identity

- Set the brand colours and heading/body fonts.
- Describe the tone in ordinary language, with a short approved example.
- Choose the language and spelling convention.
- Record how people and professional titles should be named.
- Set claims guidance and banned words relevant to this business.
- Add its confirmed public social profile links.

Save the form and reopen it to check the retained values. A message to an assistant is not a substitute for saving these settings.

For health work, use the correct business compliance profile and have the responsible person review claims guidance. A brand kit cannot grant permission for a treatment claim or replace evidence. For several businesses, check the selected business before changing anything: a clinic's rules should not become another client's defaults.
