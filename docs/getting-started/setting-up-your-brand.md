# Setting Up Your Brand
*Tell your agency about your business so it can do great work*

Your brand profile is the foundation of everything your agency creates. The more it knows about you, the better the content, strategy, and advice.

## The easy way — just talk

You do not need to fill in forms. Just tell your Director about your business in conversation. It will automatically save the details — your name, niche, audience, tone of voice, and more.

> **Tip:** Say something like "My brand is called Bloom Skincare, we sell natural skincare products for sensitive skin, mostly women aged 25-45 who care about clean ingredients."

## Automatic scanning

If you provide your website URL, the Director will scan it and fill in details automatically — your business name, what you do, your existing messaging, and more. It can also scan your GitHub repo and social media profiles.

## What your agency learns about you

Over time, your brand profile grows to include:

1. Business name, niche, and description

2. Target audience and buyer personas

3. Brand voice and tone (formal, casual, witty, etc.)

4. Content pillars — the main topics you talk about

5. Compliance flags (AHPRA/TGA for health businesses)

6. Competitor list

7. Social media profiles and connections

8. Post signature (your branding on every post)
