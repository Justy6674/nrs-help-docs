# Use From Claude Desktop
*Connect your agency to the Claude Desktop app on Mac or Windows*

You can use your NotRealSmart agency directly inside the Claude Desktop app. This means you can talk to your Director, publish posts, generate images, and run audits without opening a browser.

## How to connect

Claude Desktop uses a secure login flow (like signing into an app with Google). You do not need to copy any passwords or codes.

1. Open Claude Desktop and go to Settings.

2. Click "Add custom connector" or "Integrations".

3. Enter the server URL: https://www.notrealsmart.com.au/api/mcp

4. A browser window will open asking you to log in to NotRealSmart.

5. Log in with your usual email and password.

6. Click "Authorise" to give Claude Desktop access to your agency.

7. You will be redirected back to Claude Desktop — you are now connected.

> **Tip:** You only need to do this once. Claude Desktop will remember your connection.

## What you can do

Once connected, you can ask Claude to do anything your Director can do. For example: "Ask my NotRealSmart Director to write a blog post about winter skincare tips for Downscale." Claude will use your agency tools behind the scenes.

## Troubleshooting

If the login window does not appear, make sure you are running the latest version of Claude Desktop. If your connection expires, simply remove the connector in Settings and add it again.
