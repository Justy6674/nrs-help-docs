# Reviewing work with people

The earlier agent “meeting room” is not the current NRS workflow. Review is organised around the work and the people responsible for it.

## Reviewing a post

Open **Approvals**, select the relevant item and inspect the business, platform text, media and flags. Use **Comment** to leave context, **Request changes** to explain what must change, or **Approve** when your role allows it and the work is ready. The timeline keeps the review record.

## Reviewing a report

Open the client's report run, inspect source coverage and verification concerns, then edit the narrative where necessary. Use the full preview to inspect the entire report, including appendices. [Review, edit and send](../reports/review-edit-and-send.md) explains the separate delivery step.

State the decision plainly: what was checked, what needs changing and who will make the final decision. Several AI opinions are not several independent professional approvals. Keep unresolved health findings with the responsible human reviewer.
