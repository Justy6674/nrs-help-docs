# Quick Actions
*One-click buttons that appear below the chat box*

Below your chat input, you will see coloured action buttons. These are quick actions — one-click shortcuts that send a pre-written message to your Director.

## They change based on context

The quick actions shown depend on what is happening. When you first start, you might see "Run a marketing audit", "Fill my calendar", or "Review my brand". After a conversation about content, you might see actions related to scheduling or publishing.

## The action bar on messages

Every substantial response from your Director includes an action bar with 10 buttons:

1. Save — store the output in your content library

2. Email Me — send the content to your email

3. Send to... — share with a team member

4. Baseline — mark this as a benchmark to compare against later

5. Re-analyse — ask the Director to review and improve

6. Todo — extract action items and create tasks

7. Copy — copy the text to your clipboard

8. Remember — tell the Director to remember specific details

9. Full View — see the full output in a larger view

10. PDF — download as a PDF document

> **Tip:** The "Save" button is especially useful. Everything you save goes to your content library in the Creative Studio, where you can find it later.
