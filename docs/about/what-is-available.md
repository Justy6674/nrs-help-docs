# What is available and what is still developing

NRS is both a human-led agency and a developing product. A catalogue offer, source-code implementation and verified live client workflow are different things.

| Area | How to interpret availability |
|---|---|
| Agency services | Scoped and delivered through an agreed engagement |
| NRS desk | Capabilities depend on the deployed workspace, role and connected accounts |
| Eve and external AI | Configured, scoped assistance; provider and client compatibility need checking |
| Full referenced reporting | Implemented workflow with review and delivery controls; live acceptance must be checked for the release |
| Canva advanced round trip | Limited by integration approval and preview-API requirements |
| Abe governance work | Human-led services alongside beta/build/planned capabilities |
| Clinical decision support | Not an active NRS clinical service |
| Standalone subscriber product | Not the current agency sale |

These guides were checked against source and product decisions on **8 September 2026**. That date is not a claim that every illustrated route was tested in production, that a provider accepted a request or that the founders personally approved every sentence.

If a guide and your workspace differ, record the business, screen and task and [contact NRS](../troubleshooting/contact-support.md). Do not substitute a retired command or a different account to force the result.

The service reference gives scope and commercial distinctions. Your quote and the actual acceptance record determine what is delivered for your engagement.
