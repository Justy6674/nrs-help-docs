# Use the content calendar

Open **Calendar** and confirm the business and visible week before interpreting an empty view. A different week can hide work that still exists. Use **previous**, **this week**, and **next** to change the displayed week.

The columns show the same Monday-to-Sunday dates for every business you can access. Each business's posts and posting times use its own time zone. A Sunday evening post in Perth belongs to Sunday, even when it is already Monday in Sydney.

Use **Group by business** to switch between business rows and a merged week. Density changes how much detail each post shows. Use **Posts** to inspect saved work and its status.

## Fill a confirmed gap

An open slot appears only when NRS can confirm that the posting time has no scheduled post. Select the slot to open Compose for that business and time. A post already at that local time fills the slot.

A time skipped when daylight saving starts does not appear as an open slot. When a local time repeats as clocks go back, NRS uses its first occurrence.

Review the platform versions and [choose the schedule](schedule-a-post.md) before scheduling new work. Posting-time settings links follow your existing account access.

## Recover an unavailable or incomplete week

If scheduled posts or posting times cannot be fully loaded, the calendar names the affected records as **unavailable** or **incomplete**. Readable saved posts remain visible. Missing records do not become zero counts or confirmed free slots.

Refresh to try the read again. You can still change weeks and inspect the posts already shown. An unavailable message does not change or remove your posts or posting times. If the message persists, [contact support](../troubleshooting/contact-support.md).

When both reads complete with no records, the calendar shows its normal empty-week guidance.

## Before changing the plan

Confirm whether an item is still an editable draft, already scheduled or already published. Do not assume moving an idea in a planning document changes a network's queue. Make the authorised change through the available NRS action and verify its result.

The calendar describes recorded work. It does not create a content strategy or prove that every scheduled post has been published.

A recurring client report has its own schedule under the client's reporting settings; it is not a social post in this calendar.
