# Manage your engagement and invoices

For agency work, Justin sets the scope and each line price after a conversation. Catalogue amounts are reference points, not the amount you agree to pay. Your issued quote lists the work, exclusions, prices, payment stages, any separately offered monthly work and the terms that apply to that version.

NRS confirms the agreed scope and payment stages in writing before issuing the quote.

## Reviewing and accepting an issued quote

Use the private link NRS sends you. Check the business name, every line and amount, GST treatment, what is excluded, stage triggers and any optional monthly work. The quote page displays the exact engagement terms saved with that offer. You can ask for changes before accepting. NRS must issue a new version for changed terms, scope or prices; the earlier version remains identifiable.

If you accept, enter your legal name and confirm the terms on that exact version. Monthly work needs a separate choice and consent. Keep your private link: it is the durable copy of the issued version and acceptance. NRS attempts an email receipt, but an email provider accepting a send does not prove arrival in your inbox.

## Invoices

Project invoices follow the stages stated in the accepted offer. Monthly work is separate and only applies if expressly accepted; each agreed service period has its own invoice. Check the period, GST and amount against your accepted quote. Contact NRS with the quote and invoice reference if anything differs. Do not pay an unexpected duplicate invoice while it is being checked.

Use the invoice or client access supplied for your engagement. Check the business, invoice reference and payment instructions. A visible internal billing screen is not a customer subscription portal.

The current console does not establish the old self-service “Manage Subscription” flow. This help centre does not promise immediate upgrades, end-of-period downgrades, a free trial or a fixed deletion period after cancellation.

## If something needs changing

Contact NRS with the client name, quote or invoice reference and requested change. Do not send card details or passwords by email. Retain the agreed response with the engagement record.

For privacy and retention questions, use the current [privacy notice](https://www.notrealsmart.com.au/privacy) and your agreed terms. A general help article should not override those documents or invent a retention commitment.
