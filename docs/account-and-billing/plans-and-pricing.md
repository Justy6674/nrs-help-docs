# Services and pricing

NRS sells human-led consulting, implementation and build work. The [108-offer catalogue](../services/index.md) is the reference for the available service lines. Your agreed quote defines the work being bought, including deliverables, assumptions, exclusions and price.

An offer in the catalogue does not mean a fully automated console feature exists. Some work is delivered directly by Bec and Justin; some uses the NRS desk or other agreed tools. Healthcare and other specialist matters may need a qualified external professional.

## Before accepting work

Check the problem, intended outcome, scope, responsibilities, access needed and how completion will be assessed. Confirm any recurring work or third-party costs in the quote. If requirements change, agree the revised scope rather than assuming it is included.

The earlier promises of unlimited creation, fourteen AI specialists and a universal free trial are retired. The standalone subscriber product is not the current agency sale.

Use the current public pricing/service information and the specific quote for commercial terms; this help article does not invent a separate price or entitlement.
