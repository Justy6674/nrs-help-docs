# Share approved work

Start with the exact item and revision you intend someone to see. A private console URL, a client portal report and an exported file have different access boundaries.

## For posts and media

Use the review and export controls actually available for that item. Check the business and contents before sharing. Do not assume a copied console address is a public review link or that a person receiving it has permission to open it.

## For reports

The client portal exposes the approved, sent report snapshot to the appropriate client workspace. Raw drafts and internal delivery records remain staff work. [Review, edit and send](../reports/review-edit-and-send.md) describes how the saved report becomes the approved version.

If sending an exported document outside the portal is part of the engagement, confirm the recipient and remove unrelated material. A screenshot can reveal client names, private messages or other tabs.

An AI-generated draft should be reviewed before it is presented as the final work. Sharing does not turn an unsupported claim into a verified finding.
