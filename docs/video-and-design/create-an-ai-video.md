# Brief an AI-assisted video

AI-assisted video is a production task to agree with NRS. The current desk does not promise the old one-command avatar generator or an automatic finished video for every request.

Prepare a brief with the audience, message, platform, duration and approved facts. Decide whether the video uses real footage, graphics, a voiceover or a synthetic presenter. Identify the assets and permissions required.

## Review in stages

Approve the script before production, then review the actual video. Check spoken wording, captions, imagery, music rights and any implication that a synthetic person is a real customer or clinician.

A correct script does not guarantee the generated visual is correct. Product appearance, logos and treatment imagery need a separate check. Health promotional content must go through the responsible human reviewer.

Agree the tool, cost and deliverable in the engagement. Once a usable file exists, [upload and repurpose it](upload-and-repurpose.md) through the same media, draft and approval process as other content.
