# Multi-Scene Videos
*Create videos with different scenes, backgrounds, and scripts*

For longer or more complex videos, you can create multi-scene projects. Each scene can have a different background, avatar, and script — giving you the flexibility to tell a story or walk through multiple points.

## When to use multi-scene

Multi-scene videos are ideal for:

1. Product walkthroughs with different features highlighted in each scene

2. Tutorial videos that cover multiple steps

3. Marketing videos that tell a story (problem, solution, result)

4. Longer YouTube content that needs visual variety

## How to create one

Ask your Director to build a multi-scene video:

1. Describe the overall video and mention you want multiple scenes

2. For example: "Create a 2-minute product demo with 4 scenes — intro, features, testimonials, and call to action"

3. Your Director will write a script for each scene and show you the breakdown before generating

> **Tip:** You can review and edit each scene individually before the video is generated. Take a moment to check the scripts — it is much easier to change text than to re-render a video.

## Customising each scene

For each scene, you can specify:

1. A different background (office, outdoor, studio, or a custom image)

2. A different avatar or talking photo

3. The script and pacing for that scene

4. Transition style between scenes

## Output

The finished video stitches all scenes together into a single file. You can publish it directly to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram Reels, or any connected platform. It is also saved to your media library for future use.
