# Your standard clip: where it goes in a reel

A standard clip is a short branded video you put in many reels, such as a two second logo or product shot with a
sound. You choose yours in **Brand kit**, under **Standard clips**.

NRS gives advice on where the clip goes and how long it is. It is advice only: you can put the clip anywhere, and
NRS never stops you.

## Where to put it

| Place | Advice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Straight after the hook | Recommended, and what NRS does unless you choose otherwise | The hook earns people's attention first, TikTok puts your brand promise straight after the hook, and your brand still shows early, which Meta and Google ask for. |
| At the end | Recommended for reels of 15 seconds or more, and for ones that close with a call to action | TikTok and Google both say to end with a call to action, and a branded sign-off can carry it. The 15 second line is NRS's reading of TikTok's 9 to 15 second checklist, not a number the platforms give. In a shorter reel, more people see the clip straight after the hook. |
| In the middle | Fine | It interrupts the story you are telling. Straight after the hook or at the end are the places the platforms point to. |
| Before the hook (at the very start) | Advised against | People decide in the first three seconds whether to keep watching, so those seconds belong to the hook that gives them a reason to stay. |

The hook is your first spoken line. NRS finds it from the words heard in your clips. If no words were heard yet,
NRS treats the first three seconds as the hook. A reel with nothing else in it yet has no hook to place the clip
around, and NRS says so.

## How long it should be

NRS advises about 2 seconds, enough for a logo and a sound. Past about 3 seconds the clip holds back what the hook
promised, and Google says to get to the heart of the story faster, so NRS advises making it shorter. The 2 and 3
second figures are NRS's own, not numbers the platforms give. If NRS has not measured a clip's length yet, it says so rather than guessing.

## If you do not have one yet

Brand kit suggests making one: about 2 seconds of your logo or product, with a sound or your brand name said out
loud. Upload it in **Media**, then tick it under **Brand kit**, **Standard clips**.

## Where you see the advice

- **Brand kit, Standard clips** shows whether you have one, whether it is too long, and where it goes in a reel.
- In the reel editor, the **Standard clip** panel shows the advice next to **After the hook**, **At the playhead**
  and **At the end**, and says where the playhead is now. After you put the clip in, the message tells you what the
  advice is for that place. **Undo** takes it out.
- **Make it great** offers your standard clip straight after the hook, with the same reasons and the pages they come
  from.
- Your AI gets the same words. When it puts the clip in, NRS returns the advice for where it landed, with each page
  and the day it was read.

## Where the advice comes from

Each rule names the page it rests on. NRS read them on 26 Nov 2026.

- TikTok for Business, [TikTok Creative Made Simple](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/SMB_Creative_Playbook_External.pdf):
  the first 3 to 6 seconds are crucial, so open with a hook; after the hook, show your selling points and brand
  promise; end with a clear call to action.
- TikTok for Business, [7 Top Tips for Making TikTok Videos](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/7TopCreativeTips.pdf):
  shorter videos perform better, with 9 to 15 seconds on its checklist; sound is essential; state your business or
  product name clearly.
- Instagram for Creators, [Frequently asked questions](https://creators.instagram.com/faq): make the first three
  seconds of your reel engaging so people do not move on.
- Meta for Business, [Tailored campaigns](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/automation/tailored-campaigns): capture
  attention and include key messaging or branding within the first three seconds; design for sound on.
- Google Ads Help, [About the ABCDs of effective video ads](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14783551):
  get to the heart of the story faster, show your brand early and throughout, reinforce it with audio, and include a
  call to action.
- Think with Google, [Video advertising tips for Shorts](https://business.google.com/en-all/think/search-and-video/short-form-video-advertising-tips/):
  capture attention immediately, and keep branding natural and part of the story.
