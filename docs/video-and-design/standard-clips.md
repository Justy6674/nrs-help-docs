# Your standard clip: where it goes in a reel

A standard clip is a short branded video you put in many reels, such as a two second logo or product shot with a
sound. You choose yours in **Brand kit**, under **Standard clips**.

NRS follows what each network says. The networks do not all agree, so NRS gives each network you are posting to its
own advice, with the page it comes from. It is advice only: you can put the clip anywhere, and NRS never stops you.

## The networks differ

- **TikTok and Instagram: hook first.** Both say the first seconds decide whether people keep watching, so those
  seconds belong to your hook. TikTok puts your brand promise straight after the hook.
- **Facebook and YouTube Shorts: show the brand in the first seconds.** Meta asks for your branding within the first
  three seconds. Google asks for your brand from the start.

Straight after a hook of under three seconds suits all four, so that is where NRS puts the clip unless you choose
otherwise.

## Where to put it, network by network

| Place | TikTok and Instagram | Facebook and YouTube Shorts |
|---|---|---|
| Straight after the hook | Recommended | Recommended when the hook ends inside three seconds. After a longer hook it works, but your brand shows late. |
| At the end | Recommended for reels of 15 seconds or more and ones that close with a call to action. Fine in a shorter reel, where more people see it straight after the hook. | Works as a sign-off with your call to action, but on its own your brand shows late. |
| In the middle | Fine. It interrupts the story you are telling. | Fine, but your brand shows late. |
| Before the hook (at the very start) | Advised against | Recommended. Keep it short so your hook still lands early. |

The hook is your first spoken line. NRS finds it from the words heard in your clips. If no words were heard yet, NRS
treats the first three seconds as the hook. The 15 second line is NRS's own reading of TikTok's 9 to 15 second
checklist, not a number the platforms give.

## Which networks the advice is for

In the reel editor the advice follows the accounts ticked under **Export**. The **Standard clip** panel says which
networks it is advising for. If no account is ticked, it gives every network's advice. Networks that agree share one
line, such as "TikTok and Instagram: before the hook is advised against." When the ones you ticked disagree, the panel
says so in a sentence.

## Each network's length limit

NRS posts through Zernio, which delivers videos of these lengths:

| Network | Shortest | Longest |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 3 seconds | 10 minutes |
| Instagram Reels | 3 seconds | 15 minutes |
| Facebook Reels | 3 seconds | 60 seconds |
| YouTube Shorts | 1 second | 3 minutes. Past that, YouTube treats it as an ordinary video, not a Short. |

If adding your clip takes a reel past a network's limit, NRS tells you for that network and says how much to trim. The
clip still goes in.

## How long the clip should be

NRS advises about 2 seconds, enough for a logo and a sound. Past about 3 seconds the clip holds back what the hook
promised, and TikTok says shorter videos perform better, so NRS advises making it shorter. The 2 and 3 second figures
are NRS's own, not numbers the platforms give. If NRS has not measured a clip's length yet, it says so rather than
guessing.

## If you do not have one yet

Brand kit suggests making one: about 2 seconds of your logo or product, with a sound or your brand name said out
loud. Upload it in **Media**, then tick it under **Brand kit**, **Standard clips**.

## Where you see the advice

- **Brand kit, Standard clips** shows whether you have one, whether it is too long, and where it goes for every
  network.
- In the reel editor, the **Standard clip** panel shows the advice for your ticked networks next to **After the
  hook**, **At the playhead** and **At the end**, and says where the playhead is now. After you put the clip in, the
  message gives the verdict for each network. **Undo** takes it out.
- **Make it great** offers your standard clip straight after the hook, with each network's reasons and the pages they
  come from.
- Your AI gets the same words. It can tell NRS which networks the reel is for. When it puts the clip in, NRS returns
  each network's advice for where it landed, with each page and the day it was read.

## Where the advice comes from

NRS read these pages on 26 Nov 2026. Most of what the platforms say about where a brand goes is on their advertising
pages; their pages for ordinary posts do not say.

- TikTok Business Help Centre (ads), [Creative best practices for performance ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/creative-best-practices?lang=en):
  put your hook in the first 6 seconds, introduce what you offer in the first 3 seconds, and end with a clear call to
  action.
- TikTok for Business, [TikTok Creative Made Simple](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/SMB_Creative_Playbook_External.pdf):
  the first 3 to 6 seconds are crucial, so open with a hook; after the hook, show your selling points and brand
  promise; end with a clear call to action.
- TikTok for Business, [7 Top Tips for Making TikTok Videos](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/7TopCreativeTips.pdf):
  shorter videos perform better, with 9 to 15 seconds on its checklist; sound is essential; state your business or
  product name clearly.
- Instagram for Creators, [Frequently asked questions](https://creators.instagram.com/faq): make the first three
  seconds of your reel engaging so people do not move on.
- Meta for Business (ads), [Tailored campaigns](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/automation/tailored-campaigns):
  capture attention and include key messaging or branding within the first three seconds; design for sound on.
- Google Ads Help (ads), [About the ABCDs of effective video ads](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14783551):
  introduce your brand or product from the start and keep it there; for Shorts, brand in a way that feels part of the
  story and include a clear call to action.
- Think with Google (ads), [Video advertising tips for Shorts](https://business.google.com/en-all/think/search-and-video/short-form-video-advertising-tips/):
  capture attention immediately, and keep branding natural and part of the story.
- YouTube Help, [Understand three-minute YouTube Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877): square or
  vertical videos up to three minutes are Shorts.
- Zernio media requirements for [TikTok](https://docs.zernio.com/platforms/tiktok/media),
  [Instagram](https://docs.zernio.com/platforms/instagram/media), [Facebook](https://docs.zernio.com/platforms/facebook/media)
  and [YouTube](https://docs.zernio.com/platforms/youtube/media): the lengths in the table above.
