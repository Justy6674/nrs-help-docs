# Compliance, governance & safety

Governance work turns obligations into practical responsibilities, evidence and review steps. It supports the people accountable for the business; a scan or AI answer does not certify compliance.

## Before you start

Identify the industry, jurisdiction, people responsible, relevant systems and the material to review. Use approved, redacted examples and identify the primary regulatory sources.

## Agree the work

Agree the exact obligations and workflows covered, evidence required, responsible reviewer and any need for independent legal or professional advice. Clinical governance and public advertising are related but separate questions.

## Review the result

Check that findings quote the material at issue, cite the applicable source and state what remains uncertain. Keep the decision, reviewer and reviewed version with the resulting policy or change.
