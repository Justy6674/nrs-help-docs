# AI receptionist / after-hours capture

**NRS-048 · $3,200 · AUD, excluding GST**

This is a service reference for discussing and agreeing the work with NRS. The title and fee come from the approved catalogue; the written engagement defines the deliverables and acceptance.

## What to know

For healthcare, agree an administrative role and an escalation path. Capturing an enquiry does not authorise a bot to diagnose, triage or give treatment advice.

## Prepare for the conversation

Describe the current manual process, its exceptions, source systems, expected volume and the person who handles failures. Use synthetic examples for initial testing.

## Confirm the scope

Agree what the system may read and change, which actions need approval, how repeated requests are handled, how failures are noticed and how to stop the workflow. Document ongoing provider costs.

## Review and accept

Test the normal path, duplicate requests, missing data, expired access and partial failure. AI output needs validation before it becomes a customer message or an operational decision.

## Health and professional responsibilities

Keep personal or clinical records in the approved system. Regulatory assistance needs a responsible person to review the actual facts and current sources. [Ahpra advertising guidance](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/Resources/Advertising-hub/Advertising-guidelines-and-other-guidance/Advertising-guidelines.aspx), [TGA prescription advertising guidance](https://www.tga.gov.au/resources/guidance/complying-restrictions-advertising-prescription-medicines-public) and the [Australian Privacy Principles](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles) are reference points; their applicability depends on the work.
