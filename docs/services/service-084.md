# Practice transformation

**NRS-084 · From $28,000 · AUD, excluding GST**

This is a service reference for discussing and agreeing the work with NRS. The title and fee come from the approved catalogue; the written engagement defines the deliverables and acceptance.

## Prepare for the conversation

Bring the business goals, affected teams and locations, existing systems, constraints and decision makers. Identify work that must continue safely during changes.

## Confirm the scope

Agree phases, deliverables, milestones, acceptance, training, dependencies and budget before starting. A from-price is a starting point; the proposal defines the final scope and fee.

## Review and accept

Check each milestone with the people who use the process. Test integrations, access boundaries and recovery, and confirm handover before treating the programme as complete.
