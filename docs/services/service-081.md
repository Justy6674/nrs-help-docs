# Advisory hour

**NRS-081 · $420 · AUD, excluding GST**

This is a service reference for discussing and agreeing the work with NRS. The title and fee come from the approved catalogue; the written engagement defines the deliverables and acceptance.

## Prepare for the conversation

Bring the systems and business areas that need ongoing attention, their current condition, known risks and the person who sets priorities.

## Confirm the scope

Agree included work, capacity, response arrangements, reporting, exclusions and changes to scope. Confirm notice and cancellation terms in the engagement; a monthly catalogue price does not activate billing.

## Review and accept

Review completed work against the agreed priorities and actual outcomes. Record unresolved dependencies and the next actions rather than treating activity counts as business results.
