# Competitor analysis — up to three competitors, side by side

**NRS-101 · $780 · AUD, excluding GST**

This is a service reference for discussing and agreeing the work with NRS. The title and fee come from the approved catalogue; the written engagement defines the deliverables and acceptance.

## What to know

Name up to three competitors and agree comparison dimensions. Public signals can be compared; competitor revenue, private analytics and commercial performance must not be inferred from visibility alone.

## Prepare for the conversation

Bring the site, locations served, known competitors and authorised access to relevant Google or Bing accounts. Public search results and owner-only analytics are different evidence sources.

## Confirm the scope

Agree the sites, locations, queries, account data and date range to examine. Separate an audit from implementing its recommendations or managing listings over time.

## Review and accept

Expect clear source links, collection dates and a distinction between observed evidence and recommendations. Rankings, AI citations, reviews and platform verification cannot be guaranteed.
